# Question

In the figure below a cord pulls a box up along frictionless plane inclined at angle $\theta = 30.0 \degree$.
The box has mass $m = 5.00 kg$, and the force from the cord has magnitude $T = 25.0 N$.
What is the box’s acceleration a along the inclined plane?

![](/img/angled_box_and_cord.png)

# Answer

Drawing a free-body diagram for the box:

![](/img/angled_box_and_cord_fbd.png)

Calculating $\vec{F_{net,x}}$:

$$
\vec{F_{net,x}} = \vec{T} + \vec{Fg,x} = 25 + F_g \cdot \sin (30 \degree) = 25 + 5 \cdot (-9.8) \cdot \sin (30) = 0.5
$$

Utilizing Newton's second law:

$$
0.5 = m \cdot a = 5 \cdot a \Rightarrow a = \frac{0.5}{5} = 0.1 \frac{m}{s^2}
$$
