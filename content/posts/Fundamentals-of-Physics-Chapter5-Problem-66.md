# Problem
The figure below shows a section of a cable-car system. The maximum permissible mass of each car with occupants is 2800 kg.
The cars, riding on a support cable, are pulled by a second cable attached to the support tower on each car.
Assume that the cables are taut and inclined at angle $\theta = 35 \degree$ What is the difference in tension between adjacent sections of the pull cable if the cars are at the maximum permissible mass and are being accelerated up the incline at $0.81 m/s2$ ?

![](/img/cable_car.png)

![](/img/cable_car_fbd.png)

Let's start composing the $F_{net}$. For the $x$ axis:
$$
F_{net,x} = T - T_2 - F_{g,x} = m \cdot a
$$

$$
= T - T_2 - (m \cdot g \cdot \sin (35)) = 2800 \cdot 0.81
$$

$$
= T - T_2 = 2268 + 15738 = 18006\ N
$$
